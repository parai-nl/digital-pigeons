# The Signaling Pigeon

This form never speaks human language. She sits at the table, listens along and
now and then makes a sound as soon as the conversation comes near her: pigeons,
their food, the places where they sit and nest, intervening in their numbers,
or the city they live in. She has the lowest bar of the three pigeons. What the
sound means is for the people at the table to make of it.

The internally versioned source-to-theme catalogue is a design interpretation
of research about pigeons. It does not establish what any individual bird
thinks or what a particular recording means, and it is not a burden of proof a
sound has to clear: the pigeon signals that a subject is coming close, she
establishes nothing. The manual demonstration remains available.

No meaning label is attached to a sound. The participant stage gives no
thematic, emotional or approval/disapproval explanation of the sound, and no
words are added to it. Recordings and visual media have their own delivery and
validation status; temporary sounds are not scientific recordings.
